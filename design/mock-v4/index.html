TurnTable: two new directions

Both leave the felt-and-brass table behind. Each has a home page and a chess page you can click through: pieces move freely on the mock board (no rule checks) so you can watch how each design shows whose turn it is.

Direction AOverprint

A two-colour print job. Each player is one ink; where they meet, they overprint. Loud, flat and typographic.

Turn
Your band fills with your ink and your name stretches to full width. The board's shadow slides to your side.
Type
Archivo at three widths, Spline Sans Mono for scores and moves.
Home
Two discs swap places once on arrival. Name both players before you pick a game.
Extra
"Sitting opposite" turns the far player's name to face them, for a tablet lying flat.
Home pageChess page

Direction BPencil & Paper

A maths notebook. Half of these games began on squared paper, so the site is drawn on it: one blue pen, one red pen.

Turn
A ring is drawn round the name of the player to move. The last move is marked in highlighter.
Type
Shantell Sans, a marker hand that stays readable at small sizes.
Home
A doodle for each of the 26 games, sorted by binder tabs. Names go on dotted lines.
Extra
Wins are kept as tally marks. The board is a separate sheet, taped down a little crooked.
