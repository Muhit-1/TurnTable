Design guide

TurnTable keeps the card hovers and animations you already chose, and takes its look from your game-board site: a warm tavern table, brass and parchment, gems for players, and boards that feel like real pieces of a set. This page is the reference for Phase 3 — every colour, font, component and motion rule, with live demos.

What is kept exactly as it was: the card lift + hard shadow, the white panel that shrinks to reveal the game preview, the name fading in, the preview animations that only play on hover, the filter-pill and button hover shadows, the back-link nudge, the modal close and play-button hovers. They are copied byte-for-byte into preserved-hub.css / preserved-game.css, and check-motion.mjs fails if a single value drifts.

01

Principles

Four ideas decide every choice below.

One table, many games

A single set of materials

Every board sits in the same wooden frame with a brass inlay, on the same table. Switching games feels like swapping the board, not the room.

Warm, never flat

Gradients and soft shadows

Pieces and players are materials — gems, glossy discs, lacquered stones — not flat colour swaps. Surfaces are tinted by the table wood.

Motion has a job

Say what changed

A disc drops, a turn banner slides in, a win pops. Hover previews teach what a game is. Nothing moves just to move, and everything respects reduced-motion.

Keep the signature

The card system stays

The chunky cards and their hover choreography are TurnTable’s own. They are the one thing deliberately not restyled by the tavern theme.

06

Board look

One frame, many boards. Each game keeps its own surface — felt, paper, lacquered wood, painted squares — inside the same walnut frame with a brass inlay and corner studs, so the set feels like it came out of one box.

Recipe

Frame

18px walnut frame with a faint grain, an inner 2px brass inlay 9px in from the edge, four brass corner studs, and a deep table-tinted drop shadow. Radius 22px. The same .board-frame wraps every game.

Surface & pieces

The playing surface is inset (inner shadow) and styled per game. Pieces use radial gradients with a highlight and a contact shadow. Legal moves glow brass; the last move gets a soft ring; wins pulse.

Rules for both

Do
  • Keep touch targets 44px or larger on boards.
  • Show whose turn it is in two ways (glow + words).
  • Let the table colour drive every panel and shadow.
  • Use brass for the one thing that matters now.
Don’t
  • Restyle the card shell, panel, or preview animations.
  • Rely on colour alone — players also differ by name and position.
  • Cover the winning line with a pop-up for longer than a few seconds.
  • Add new motion without a reduced-motion rule.
