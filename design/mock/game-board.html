    Connect Four

    Objective

    Be the first to connect 4 of your coloured discs in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

    Setup

    Stand the 7-column × 6-row grid upright; each player takes one disc colour.

    How to play

    Players alternate turns dropping one disc into the top of any column; it falls to the lowest open slot in that column.

    How to win

    First to get 4 in a row wins; if the grid fills with no 4-in-a-row, it’s a draw.
