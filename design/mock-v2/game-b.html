    How to play Chess

    Objective
    Checkmate the opponent's king: trap it under attack with no legal escape.
    Setup
    Each side starts with 16 pieces on an 8×8 board; white is at the bottom and moves first.
    Turns
    Players alternate moves. Select a piece, then a square it can reach.
    Winning
    Checkmate wins. Stalemate or a draw by rule ends the game level.
